The main room, most nights around 7pm.

A workshop we didn't fully renovate.

We kept the brick, the ductwork, the bones of the building. What we added hangs from it — strings of copper bulbs at table height, so the light sits close and low and warm.

Tables are reclaimed timber, seating is close together on purpose, and one wall stays given over to the plants because a room this industrial needs something alive in it.
